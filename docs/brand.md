```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: final standalone app icon for “赛探 AI / SaiTan AI”, a Chinese university competition discovery app powered by AI browsing. This icon will replace the existing generic orbit icon in a dark navy and violet website header, footer and browser favicon.
Primary request: design ONE distinctive, polished, professional brand symbol combining an elegant flowing S-shaped race/path ribbon and a small four-point discovery star at its upper right. The S should read clearly and suggest exploration, forward motion and finding opportunities. It should be a strong custom geometric silhouette, not a stock orbit or a magnifying glass.
Composition: a single centered square rounded app tile, front-facing flat graphic, occupying approximately 88% of the square canvas, with a modest transparent margin outside its rounded silhouette. No presentation board or multiple concepts.
Style: restrained modern vector-like logo art with clean precise edges, thick legible shapes and clear negative space, suitable at 32px. Rich violet to periwinkle tile (#6550da to #9b83f5), an ivory/white S-shaped ribbon, and one pale mint discovery star (#d7ffbc). Very subtle depth within the tile only, no glassy 3D, no bevelled metallic surfaces.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha outside the rounded tile, no surrounding color, no fake checkerboard.
Text: NO text, NO letters outside the S-shaped symbol, no Chinese wordmark, no tagline.
Constraints: exactly one icon; no mockup, no watermark, no extra decorative speckles, no trophy, no gradients in the surrounding background. The result is the production logo image itself.
```
